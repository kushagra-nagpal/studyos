# Copy/paste sample inputs

All examples are synthetic. Replace details with your own course material.

## Planner
“Exam: [date]. Available study blocks: [list]. Topics and confidence: [topic — low/medium/high]. Known exam format: [format]. Create a feasible plan with breaks, buffer, and a catch-up rule. Mark assumptions.”

## Notes
“Turn the following material into revision notes. Preserve definitions and conditions. Separate source-backed facts from inferences and list anything to verify. Put self-test questions before a separate answer key. Source: [paste notes].”

## Active recall
“From this source, make 8 retrieval questions: 2 recall, 2 explain-why, 2 apply/trace, 1 compare, 1 diagnose. Show questions first and answers in a separate key. Flag any question requiring knowledge not in the source. Source: [paste].”

## Socratic tutor
“I am trying to understand [concept]. Start by checking my current understanding. Ask one question at a time and use hints before giving a full solution. My course's definition/notation is: [if known].”

## Error diagnosis
“Here is the problem, my attempt, and expected behavior. Find the earliest meaningful error; if information is missing, ask rather than guess. Explain with a small counterexample, then give me a hint before the full fix and a new transfer problem. Problem: […]. Attempt: […].”

## Exam strategy
“Use only the syllabus, exam format, past papers, and confidence information below. Separate confirmed facts from historical patterns and uncertainty. Create a timed rehearsal whose durations add up; do not claim to predict the exam. Materials: […].”
